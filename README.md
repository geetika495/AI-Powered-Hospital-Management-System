# 🏥 AI-Powered Hospital Management System

An intelligent healthcare automation platform built using **n8n**, **Google Gemini AI**, **Supabase**, and **Gmail** to automate patient registration, appointment booking, AI-assisted medical report analysis, doctor approvals, medication reminders, and billing.

---

# 🚀 Features

- Patient Registration
- Appointment Booking
- AI Medical Report Analysis
- Doctor Approval Workflow
- Medication Reminder System
- Billing & Hospital Analytics
- Email Notifications
- Human-in-the-loop Approval
- AI Decision Support

# 🔄 Workflow Overview

## Workflow 1
Patient Registration

- Register patient
- Duplicate check
- Generate Patient ID
- Store in Supabase
- Send confirmation email

---

## Workflow 2
Appointment Booking

- Receive symptoms
- AI predicts department
- Find doctor
- Book appointment
- Email confirmation

---

## Workflow 3
Medical Report Upload & AI Analysis

- Upload PDF
- Extract report
- Gemini AI analysis
- Generate summary
- Store report

---

## Workflow 4
Doctor Approval

- Doctor reviews AI recommendation
- Approve / Reject
- Update database
- Notify patient

---

## Workflow 5
Medication Reminder

- Scheduled reminders
- Follow-up tracking
- Email notifications

---

## Workflow 6
Billing & Hospital Analytics

- Generate invoice
- Calculate bill
- Store invoice
- Revenue analytics
